# Design System: Sighthound Content Relay

> **This file is a summary, not the authority.** `design-system/` is the canonical source for brand tokens, type, and assets (see "Design System Authority" in `AGENTS.md`). Token changes start in `design-system/colors_and_type.css` and flow into `src/app/globals.css`. If this file and `design-system/` disagree, `design-system/` wins and this file is stale. It was generated by `/impeccable document` from the code on 2026-11-15; the North Star name and rule names below are proposed wording, not yet reviewed by the team.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Calm Relay Desk"**

Content Relay is a working tool for a small content team that moves blogs and social posts through writing, review, and publishing. The interface behaves like a well-run dispatch desk: quiet white and cool-gray surfaces, dark navy ink, and a single Blurple signal that marks what is interactive, selected, or in focus. Colour beyond that is reserved for workflow status, where it carries meaning.

Density is app density, not marketing density. Body text is 14px, tables are compact, and controls use an 8px radius. The brand's 16px scale and 20px pill buttons belong to the login screen and other marketing-style surfaces only. The brand should be recognisable through Lexend, navy ink, and Blurple precision, with no imagery needed.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Calm, technical, enterprise-grade; approachable without being playful.
- Navy ink on white and cool gray; never pure black, never arbitrary slate for text.
- One accent (Blurple) for interaction, selection, focus, and links.
- Semantic status colours stay separate from the brand palette.
- Lucide icons at 1.75 stroke through the shared `AppIcon` system; no emoji in the UI.

## Colors

A cool, restrained palette: roughly 60% Blurple-led brand presence in marketing, but in this app the neutrals do most of the work and Blurple marks intent.

### Primary
- **Blurple** (`blurple`): primary buttons, links, focus border, selected-tile border. Hover deepens to `blurple-700`; brand badge text uses `blurple-800`.
- **Blurple tints** (`blurple-50`, `blurple-100`): hover wash on secondary and ghost controls, focused input background, selected tile and pill fills, info cards.

### Secondary
- **Dark Navy** (`navy`): all primary text and headings, the active sidebar item fill, tooltips.
- **Navy mid tones** (`navy-500`, `navy-700`): secondary and meta text, table headers, inactive nav labels, tooltip border.

### Tertiary
- **Warm accents** (`orange-light`, `orange-medium`, `orange-red`): brand pops only, capped at about 10% of any composition. Never used as fills for large areas or as workflow status colours.

### Neutral
- **Neutral Gray** (`gray`): the app canvas and muted card surface; also the table row hover.
- **Gray ramp** (`gray-200`, `gray-400`, `gray-600`): default borders, hover borders, skeleton base, tertiary text.
- **White** (`white`): cards, inputs, the sidebar, raised surfaces.

### Semantic status (outside the brand palette)
Workflow status chips use Tailwind's semantic families and are locked by the Global Vocabulary Contract in `AGENTS.md`: slate (not started, draft, planned), blue (in progress, writing), violet (in review, pending review), rose (needs revision, changes requested), sky (ready to publish), amber (awaiting live link), emerald (approved, completed, published). Danger actions use rose; warnings use amber; success uses emerald. The source is `src/lib/status.ts`.

### Named Rules
**The Navy Ink Rule.** Text is Dark Navy or `navy-500`. Never `#000`, never slate or gray utility classes for body copy.

**The Status-Is-Not-Brand Rule.** Never remap a workflow status to Blurple, Navy, or Orange. Status colour and brand colour are two separate systems.

**The No-Raw-Hex Rule.** Components consume tokens (`bg-brand`, `text-ink`, `var(--sh-gray-200)`). Brand hex values are not typed into component code.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Lexend (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, Verdana)
**Body Font:** Lexend (same stack)
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono, for IDs, slugs, and technical values

**Character:** One family at a narrow weight range (400, 500, 600). Hierarchy comes from size and weight, not from a second typeface. Body copy carries a slight negative tracking (-0.01em).

### Hierarchy
- **Page title** (600, 24px, snug): one per page, via `.page-title`.
- **Section title** (600, 18px, snug): major groupings on a page.
- **Subsection label / card title** (600, 16px, 1.5): minor headings.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 24px line height): default text, list items, toasts.
- **Meta / caption / form helper** (400, 12px, 16px line height, `navy-500`): secondary information.
- **Table header** (600, 12px, `navy-500`): column labels.
- **Mono technical** (JetBrains Mono, 12px): IDs, slugs, codes.

Numbers in tables and counts use tabular figures (`.tabular-nums`).

### Named Rules
**The App Density Rule.** App body text is 14px / 400. The brand's 46px H1 and 16px body scale are for marketing and auth surfaces only.

**The One Family Rule.** Lexend everywhere except technical values. The wordmark is never typeset; use the logo files in `design-system/assets/`.

## Layout

A fixed app shell: white sidebar on the left (collapsible to icons), a top bar, and a content column on the gray canvas. Spacing follows a 4px base (`4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40`). List pages share one structure through `data-page.tsx` (header, toolbar, filters) and `data-table.tsx` (table, pagination, row limits). Below the `md` breakpoint the sidebar becomes an overlay opened from a 44px menu button. Long unbroken strings wrap outside tables; inside tables, cells truncate.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat. Depth comes from the white-on-gray surface contrast and 1px `gray-200` borders. Shadows are navy-tinted, never black, and appear on overlays and selection, not on resting cards.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **brand-xs** (`0 1px 2px rgba(26, 29, 56, 0.06)`): selected tiles.
- **brand-sm** (`0 2px 6px rgba(26, 29, 56, 0.08)`): light lift.
- **brand-md** (`0 8px 20px rgba(26, 29, 56, 0.10)`): popovers and menus.
- **brand-lg** (`0 20px 40px rgba(26, 29, 56, 0.14)`): modals and drawers.
- **brand-focus** (`0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 96, 220, 0.35)`): the focus ring on every interactive element.

### Named Rules
**The Navy Shadow Rule.** Shadows are tinted with navy (`26, 29, 56`). No black shadows.

**The One Focus Ring Rule.** Keyboard focus is always the 3px Blurple ring (`shadow-brand-focus`). Do not invent per-component focus styles.

## Shapes

Soft but not round. Dense app controls and inputs use 6 to 8px corners, cards use 12px, badges and filter pills are full pills, and only CTA buttons on marketing and auth surfaces use the 20px brand radius. Borders are 1px and light gray; the active table row is marked by a 2px inset bar on its first cell.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** compact 8px radius for `md`, `sm`, `xs`, and `icon` sizes; 20px for `cta`.
- **Primary:** Blurple fill, white text; hover `blurple-700`. One per view.
- **Secondary (default):** white fill, navy text, `gray-200` border; hover washes to `blurple-50`.
- **Ghost / Icon:** `navy-500` text; hover `blurple-50` with navy text.
- **Destructive:** rose-600 fill, used only with a confirmation modal.
- **Focus / Disabled:** Blurple focus ring; disabled at 60% opacity.
- Always use `Button` or `buttonClass()` from `src/components/button.tsx`.

### Badges and status chips
- **Badge:** full pill, 12px medium text, 2px by 8px padding, in default, muted, brand, info, critical, warning, and success tones. For roles, tags, and severity.
- **Status chip:** the locked semantic palette above. Not interchangeable with Badge.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** white (default) or Neutral Gray (muted: empty, loading, and error states); red-50, amber-50, and `blurple-50` for critical, warning, and info.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; border only.
- **Selectable tiles:** 8px radius; active state is `blurple-50` fill, Blurple border, navy text.
- **Internal Padding:** 12px by default.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white fill, 1px `gray-200` border, 6px radius, 14px text.
- **Focus:** Blurple border, `blurple-50` background, Blurple focus ring (`.focus-field`).
- **Disabled:** 60% opacity.

### Navigation
- White sidebar. Items are 44px tall, 8px radius, Lucide icon plus 14px label.
- Inactive: `navy-500`, hover `blurple-50`. Active: navy fill, white text, semibold.
- Collapsed mode shows icons only, each with an accessible label and tooltip.

### Tables
- Compact rows, 12px semibold headers in `navy-500`, hover fills the row with Neutral Gray and marks the first cell with a 2px gray bar. Row actions fade in on hover and on keyboard focus.

### Feedback surfaces
- **Tooltip:** navy bubble, white 12px medium text, 300ms delay.
- **Skeleton:** `gray-200` base with a soft white shimmer; shimmer stops under reduced motion.
- **Drawer:** slides in from the right over about 220ms with a fading overlay.

Motion is short and functional: 80 to 250ms, ease-out, used for state changes only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` all durations collapse to zero.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take tokens from `globals.css` utilities (`bg-brand`, `text-ink`, `text-navy-500`, `shadow-brand-*`).
- **Do** use `Button`, `Card`, `Badge`, `EmptyState`, `DataTable`, and the other shared primitives listed in `design-system/PRODUCTION_COMPONENT_MAP.md`.
- **Do** keep app text at 14px / 400 and controls at the 8px radius.
- **Do** keep the sidebar white.
- **Do** use Lucide icons through `AppIcon` at 1.75 stroke.
- **Do** pull user-facing labels from `src/lib/ui-vocab.ts`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use pure black text or black shadows.
- **Don't** use orange accents as fills or as status colours.
- **Don't** recolour workflow status chips with brand colours.
- **Don't** apply the 20px CTA radius or the 16px marketing type scale to dense app controls.
- **Don't** use emoji as icons.
- **Don't** recreate the Sighthound wordmark in type.
- **Don't** import files from `design-system/ui_kits/` into `src/`; they are reference only.
- **Don't** change a brand token here or in `globals.css` first; start in `design-system/colors_and_type.css`.
